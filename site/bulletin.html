The Brief

Why this page exists

Soreno is the club's shared starting point. Everyone opens the same page on the same morning, so the weekly meeting begins with a common picture of what happened instead of a cold start. It rebuilds itself before the opening bell on every trading day — researched live from the reading list, never from memory — and anyone with the link can read it, no login.
